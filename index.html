I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research interests include prompt learning, domain generalization, and representation learning for vision-language-action models.

My current projects explore generalizable action representations for embodied intelligence, including covariant action modeling, semantic-action manifold alignment, and human-to-robot transfer for robotic manipulation. Learn more about my research interests in publications.

About me

Belief. Useful embodied intelligence emerges when action representations stay stable across tasks, scenes, and embodiments.

My current work centers on VLA training and representation learning for embodied intelligence. I am interested in how vision-language-action models acquire compact, transferable action spaces that support robust manipulation and generalization.

I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, supervised by Changsheng Xu. I am broadly open to research collaborations, visiting opportunities, and industry research roles around embodied AI, robot learning, and multimodal foundation models.
